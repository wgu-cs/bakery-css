Welcome to North Star Bakery

Wrapped in the smell of warm bread and a little cinnamon, North Star Bakery has been the heartbeat of Maple Grove for more than fifteen years. We bake in small batches from before sunrise so that every loaf, pastry, and cake you take home is the freshest it can possibly be.

Pre-order now

Whether you are a commuter grabbing a coffee and a croissant, a family picking up the weekend loaf, or an event planner sourcing dessert for two hundred guests — we are glad you are here.

What we're known for

A few of the things our neighbors keep coming back for:

  • Hand-shaped sourdough built on a starter we've fed every morning since 2011.
  • Flaky laminated pastries - croissants, morning buns, and seasonal galettes.
  • Celebration cakes made to order for birthdays, weddings, and milestones.
  • Weekly seasonal specials that follow what local farms are harvesting right now.
