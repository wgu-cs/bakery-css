Our Products

Everything is baked fresh each day in small batches, so the case looks a little different every time you visit. Below is what we make most weeks, with a pricing range for each category. There is no online checkout - you can grab items at the counter, or place a pre-order to reserve what you want.

Breads

Our daily breads are built on a rye-infused sourdough starter and shaped by hand. We bake a rotating lineup, and take pre-orders for Saturday loaves.

  • Country Sourdough - an open, custardy crumb with a dark crackly crust. $7-$9 per loaf
  • Seeded Multigrain - flax, sesame, sunflower, and a hint of honey. $8-$10 per loaf
  • Olive & Rosemary Ciabatta - slipper loaves salted with Castelvetrano olives. $9-$12 per loaf
  • Saturday-night Pizza Dough - cold-fermented balls ready for your home oven. $5-$6 per ball

Pastries

Laminated three days ahead and baked through the morning rush, our pastries are the reason there's a line on weekends. Arrive early for the best selection.

  • Butter Croissants & Pain au Chocolat $4-$5 each
  • Morning Buns - cardamom and citrus, rolled in raw sugar. $4.50 each
  • Seasonal Galettes - free-form fruit tarts that change with the market. $6-$8 each
  • Kouign-Amann - caramelized Breton butter pastry, limited daily. $5.50 each

Cakes

Cakes are made to order for celebrations of every size. Because we bake from scratch in small batches, please give us at least five days' notice for custom cakes and longer for large events. See the pre-order form to start your request.

  • Everyday Layer Cakes - vanilla, chocolate, or lemon, finished in buttercream. $32-$48 (serves 8-12)
  • Custom Celebration Cakes - designed around your occasion and flavors. $60-$180
  • Wedding & Event Cakes - tiered cakes for large gatherings, by quote. Custom pricing
  • Cupcakes & Petit Fours - by the dozen for parties and offices. $3-$4 each, dozen minimum
