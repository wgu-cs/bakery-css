Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a single starter and a borrowed oven. In 2011, our founder Mae Ellison started selling loaves from a folding table at the Maple Grove farmers' market. Within a year there was a line every Saturday, and within three years we had a little shop with our name on the awning at 42 Lantern Street.

We named ourselves after the North Star because a bakery, at its best, is a fixed point a neighborhood steers toward - for a Saturday loaf, a birthday cake, or just a warm place on a cold morning. That is still what we aim to be.

Sourcing philosophy

Good bread begins with good grain. We mill a portion of our flour in-house from regionally grown wheat, and we buy the rest from small mills within a day's drive. Our dairy comes from two local creameries, our eggs from a family farm up the valley, and our seasonal fruit from the same market stalls where we got our start.

  • Stone-milled wheat from regional growers, milled fresh weekly.
  • Pasture-raised dairy and eggs from within fifty miles.
  • Seasonal produce from the Maple Grove farmers' market.
  • No artificial colors, flavors, or dough conditioners - just time and heat.
